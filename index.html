/ FAQ
Common questions
Is it free to browse VehicleOS?
+
Absolutely — browsing is completely free with no sign-up required. Create a free account to unlock saved searches, alerts, and your personal favourites list.
How often are new vehicles added?
+
Our listings are refreshed daily — over 400 new vehicles added every morning. We cover a wide range of makes, body types, fuel types and price points across the UK.
What does the condition grade mean?
+
Every vehicle carries an independently assessed condition grade — AA being the highest. This grade reflects bodywork, mechanical condition, interior quality and service history.
Can I get notified when a specific vehicle is listed?
+
Yes. Sign in and save any search with your preferred filters — make, model, mileage, location. We'll send you an email the moment a matching vehicle appears on the platform.
Are the listed prices fixed or negotiable?
+
Pricing varies by listing — some vehicles show a fixed asking price, others are marked POA (Price on Application). Click any listing for full details and contact options.
